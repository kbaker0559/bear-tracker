# Black Bear Quota Rules

## Standard decrease chart

- -10 or worse: down 4
- -6 through -9: down 3
- -3 through -5: down 2
- -1 or -2: down 1
- Even: no change

## Positive results

A positive result increases quota only when the golfer finishes in the money:

- +1 or +2: up 1
- +3 or +4: up 2
- +5 or +6: up 3
- +7 through +9: up 4
- +10 or more: up half the plus result, rounded up

A positive result outside the money does not change quota.

## In-the-money protection

A golfer who finishes in the money with an even or negative result does not go up or down.

## Horse's Ass rule

Horse's Ass winners, including ties, use the standard negative chart with a maximum decrease of two points.

- -1 or -2: down 1
- -3 or worse: down 2
- Even or positive: no change

The Horse's Ass rule never forces a larger decrease than the standard chart.
